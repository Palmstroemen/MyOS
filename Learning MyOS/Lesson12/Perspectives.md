So by means of permissions your financial department is allowed to just see the /finance/ folders and all their entries and subfolders. Wouldn't it be nice, if you could also have a concentrated view on our financial data? Or your legal affairs? On your development or communication?

So you might have the right to see all your data, but you want to focus on a specific subset of your data. That's what perspectives are made for. Build them easily yourself! Build different perspectives for different projects or use some perspectives for all of them. 

A perspective might be somehow like your Today-Screen in some apps. It presents you the most recent, the most urgent, the most interesting data on a glimpse.

A perspective might also be an overview about multiple subprojects. This is, where your naming-convention comes in handy. Want to see your open bills over all your projects?

Think of a perspective a bit like an saved search. You can do this in your OS but almost no one uses it because it's kind of weird and complicated. With the power of our standard templates and tags it becomes a total new game.
