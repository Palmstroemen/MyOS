You have just created and used your first project.
You also used some standard folders that come with this project. That's where it all starts, but not where it ends.

So let's talk about projects.
Projects are the universal organization structure here. You might even say: everything is a project.

A project is kind of an ordinary folder on steroids.
* It may have subprojects, and those may have subprojects, and so on.
* It comes with a predefined set of folders and subfolders, defined in a **Template** (we will look at this later).
* It comes with other features you will get to know over time.

But never forget: each project starts small, and maybe 90% of your projects stay small. Even a simple **Markdown.md file** can be a small project, or a **simple folder with a few files inside** can be considered a project.

# Everything is a Project
Think for a moment about your life.
## Family
There is your family with your parents, your partner, your kids... One main project "Family" with subprojects "Parents", "Partner", and "Kids", and each of those with subprojects like "Mom" and "Dad" and your kids' names respectively.
Be aware: These are not standard folders we provide. These are folders you can create and name as you like. Our standard folders are inside each of them.

# Standard Folders
When we say "standard folders" don't think of a fixed structure. We don't want to overwhelm you, but for now you only need to know that you can adapt them to your specific needs. You can even adapt them to your projects.

Think of them as a building-block system you can shape to your needs.

And there are **two more things** you need to understand for the moment:

1. We do not spam your system with standard folders the moment you turn an ordinary folder into a project. Our standard folders are more like a proposal. They are not there until you use them. The moment you drop a file into one of them, they materialize. They are like embryos that are born when you use them. We'll show you this magic soon.
2. Imagine what it means when you use the same naming conventions for folders in all your projects. This means **the machine can read** your structure. Just like the machine once knew where to find your music, it now knows where to find your communication, your finances, your documentation. That's the **game changer**. That's huge. Your computer might know where to put your bills and invoices (into /finance/ and then maybe /new invoices/). If you put your photos and videos from your vacations right into our folder /documentation/photos/, they are right where they belong.
   **That's how we humans organize ourselves.** It's YOUR structure, not the one some computer nerds think is appropriate for you. They encapsulate your data in containers because they are afraid someone could do bad things with it. But for YOU it became a nightmare to move data from one container to another: export, import, incompatibility, rebuilding structures in your CMS, your accounting software, your SAP... you name it.
