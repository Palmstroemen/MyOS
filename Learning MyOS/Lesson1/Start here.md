# Like with headings

### or with headings of various kinds

or with *italic* or **bold** text or even ***italic and bold*** text.

Let's try that.

Write your first heading like this:
'# my first heading' (without the quotes)

**BOOM! You did it!**
Now write a smaller subheading:
'### my first subheading'

**WOW! You're doing great!**
Now write this line:
'This text shall be \*italic\* and \*\*this shall be bold\*\*.'

**Maybe try this:**
Try to make "this here bold" by selecting it and typing '\*\*' on your keyboard. Nice!

You get the idea. You just learned your first steps in Markdown, which is kind of the opposite of Markup. Markdown is easy to use and very fast for capturing ideas and thoughts.
