So you have organized your vacation data into folders. That's kind of the past. Now let's have a brief look at the future.

For this lesson we put all your files and documents back into a single, unsorted folder. Sorry for that, but we did it for a good reason.

Select this folder and check the context menu (right-click). You might see something like: **Turn into a project**. Give it a try!

Now you should see "standard" folders inside your vacation folder — similar to "Music" or "Videos" in old operating systems. You have turned an ordinary folder into a project folder with a predefined set of standard folders.

Now try again to sort your files into these folders. If you wonder where to put your photos, think of your vacation in a more abstract, generalized way. What are your photos if not the **documentation** of what you did?

After you're done, proceed to [[MyOS/Learning MyOS/Lesson06/Readme|the next lesson]].
