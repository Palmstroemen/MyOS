But before we do that, let's go on vacation...
... hmm. Turns out it's quite a project to go on vacation.
1. First you collect information about where you'd like to go.
2. Then you decide.
3. Then you book flights and hotels.
4. Maybe you have conversations with a booking agency, or friends who want to join in.
5. Maybe you want to book a car as well.
6. Then there are insurance policies for flights, hotels, and car bookings.
7. Finally you're on vacation. But what happens there?
8. You take a bunch of photos.
9. Maybe you bring all your invoices and bills back home, and you do ...
10. ... a final calculation of the costs.
