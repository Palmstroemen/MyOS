# For example with headings

### or with headings of various sizes

or with *italic* or **bold** text or even ***italic and bold*** text.

**Let's try that!**
Write your first heading like this:
'**# My first heading**' *(without the quotes, and don't forget the # character!)*

<-- give it a try here!

**BOOM! You did it!**
Now write a smaller subheading:
'**### My first subheading**'

<-- give it a try here!


**WOW! You're doing great!**
Now write this line:
'**Some text shall be \*italic\* and \*\*some shall be bold\*\*.**'

<-- give it a try here!

**Maybe try this, too:**
Make "this part bold" by selecting it and typing '\*\*' on your keyboard. Nice!

You get the idea. You just took your first steps in Markdown – kind of the opposite of Markup, the language websites are written in.

Markdown is easy to use and very fast for capturing ideas and thoughts.
