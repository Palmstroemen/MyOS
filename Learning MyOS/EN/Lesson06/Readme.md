You have just created and used your first project.
You have also used some of the standard folders that come with it. That's where it all starts, but not where it ends.

So let's talk about projects.
Projects are the universal organization structure here. You might even say: everything is a project.

A project is kind of an ordinary folder on steroids.
* It may have subprojects, which may have subprojects again, and so on.
* It comes with a predefined set of folders and subfolders, defined in a **template** (we will get to that later).
* It comes with other features you will get to know over time.

But never forget: every project starts small, and maybe 95 % of your projects stay small. Even a simple **Markdown file** can be a small project, and so can a **simple folder with a few files inside**.

# Everything is a Project
Think about your life for a moment.
## Let's start with your family
There is your family: your parents, your partner, your kids ... One main project "Family" with the subprojects "Parents", "Partner", and "Kids", and each of those again with subprojects like "Mom" and "Dad" or your kids' names.
Be aware: your projects are not standard folders that we impose on you. Your projects are almost ordinary folders that you create and name yourself. But our standard folders are inside each of them as suggestions.

Now check whether the standard folders fit. You will probably always need:
* 00_Admin – administration
* 01_Info – information you collect
* 03_Communication
* 04_Finance – money matters
* 05_Legal – contracts, insurance, legal cases
* 08_Documentation – maybe the best photos and videos of a person

For people, there might be more, like:
* health
* education

We'll come to this later. And of course we don't keep you from adding ordinary folders of your own to this structure.

## Next: housing
You hopefully have an apartment, a house, or even several. Depending on your needs, you organize this in various projects and subprojects. But in each of these projects you will probably have the same topics again:
* 00_Admin
* 01_Info
* 03_Communication
* 04_Finance
* 05_Legal
* 08_Documentation

Here, too, you can add folders of your own if something is missing from the standard structure.

## Cars and transport
Think of your car, your bicycle, your public transport pass – or several of them, if you happen to have multiple cars, bikes, or motorbikes.
Do our standard folders fit? Maybe not all of them, but many. There is always money to manage. Purchase contracts, too. A few pictures that belong to it ...

## Computers, tablets, phones, routers, printers, drones ...
Whatever computer-like devices you own – the standard folders fit.

## Adventures and vacations
Your weekend trip, your summer holidays, your sabbatical ... everything is a project.

## Hobbies
Making music, repairing motorcycles, sports ... you name it – the standard folders fit.

## Jobs
Whether you have one job or several: every job has subprojects, sub-subprojects, clients ... whatever you need. Our standard folders come in handy.

# Standard Folders
When we say "standard folders", don't think of a rigid structure. We don't want to overwhelm you; for now you only need to know that you can adapt them to your needs – even differently for each project.

Think of them as a set of building blocks you shape to your liking.

And there are **two things** you should understand right now:

1. We do not flood your system with standard folders the moment you turn an ordinary folder into a project. Our standard folders are just a suggestion. They are not there until you use them. The moment you drop a file into one of them, it becomes real. They are like unborn folders that only come to life when you need them. We'll show you this magic in a moment.
2. Imagine what it means when your folders are named the same in all your projects: **the machine can read your structure.** Just like the computer once knew where your music was, it now knows where to find your communication, your finances, your documentation. That's the **game changer**. That's huge. Your computer might know where your invoices and receipts belong (in /04_Finance/ and there maybe in /Expenses/). If you put your vacation photos and videos right into /08_Documentation/Pictures/, they are exactly where they belong.
   **That's how we humans organize ourselves.** It's YOUR structure, not the one some computer nerds consider appropriate for you. They lock your data into containers because they are afraid someone could do bad things with it. But for YOU it became a nightmare to move data from one container to another: export, import, incompatibilities, rebuilding the same structure again and again – in your mail program, your photos, your CMS, your accounting, your SAP ... you name it.
