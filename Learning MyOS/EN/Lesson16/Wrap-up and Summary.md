We have reached the end of the tutorial. What have you learned about MyOS?

1. Everything, even the biggest international corporation, starts with an idea, a small note on a piece of paper. Many ideas stay small, some grow. Ideas don't stand alone: they refer to each other, they are linked. With Markdown you have the most modern and convenient way to capture your thoughts, ideas, and projects as a network.
2. When a project grows, MyOS offers you ready-made structures: folders that are always named the same and invite you to file your documents in an orderly way. The principle: everything that belongs to a project in one place. Not the mails somewhere in the mail program, the invoices somewhere in the accounting, the pictures somewhere in the Photos folder – each time with a different structure. No: everything in your project folder. You can take it along on a USB stick or in the cloud, encrypt it, and give it access rights.
3. Finally a clear filing system with consistent names that you quickly get used to. So you will find your things even after decades. And anyone who knows the system finds their way around immediately: a new employee finds their way without long training, and everyone files the same way.
4. From the very beginning, your project structure follows the organization of big companies. Your small /04_Finance/ folder might one day become the finance department of your global corporation. Or it simply stays a small folder in your project.
5. All these folders are suggestions. You can still create any folders of your own, as before. But you will be amazed how much is already offered to you, well organized.
6. You can shape these folder structures to your own needs. You don't have to adopt exactly the structure we suggest – it fits everyone a little differently. You can assemble it from building blocks or design it from scratch. Several structures can exist side by side on your computer, for example your company's project structure and your private one next to it.
7. How many /2025/07/ month folders have you created by hand? Sometimes your photo program does it for you. In the future, MyOS will do it for you.
8. Filters and perspectives give you very direct views of your data.
9. You no longer need a computer science degree to manage access rights.
10. Your desktop, your taskbar, your theme adapt to your projects, if you want. So you see at a glance whether you are in the car workshop, the music studio, or the finance department.
11. And with the folder fan, every folder is only a flick of the hand away.

Think for a moment about what this could mean.
* You get back an overview of your data. Finally you know where to file things – just as the folders Pictures, Videos, Music, Downloads, Documents once did for us, only much smarter.
* Imagine children at school learning not how to overload a presentation with animations, but how to organize themselves and their lives and stay in control of their data.
* Imagine AI knowing immediately where to find information, invoices, or mails, and where to file them. Searching for the latest invoice takes no time at all. And when a search only has to look through a tenth of the computer, it needs only a tenth of the energy. Not just for you. For all of Europe, for the whole world!
