One more thing. Imagine you like making music and have some programs for it. You are also into cars and have a few special programs for them, too. For a lot of administrative things you use the usual suspects: word processor, spreadsheet, presentations. And you like gaming and have some games on your computer.

Now imagine you walk into the car workshop on your computer and find everything you need for your cars. All programs are ready in the taskbar. And there's no word processor in your way, because you never need it in the workshop. The same in your music studio: there you don't need the programs for your cars. Of course you can start any program at any time, but they don't clutter your taskbar.

In the workshop you might like a dark theme and a desktop background with a pretty Ferrari. In the music studio you prefer working in a light theme, with an orchestra as background.

Today you could only set this up by creating a separate user for each of your interests: a user "Music Studio", one "Car Workshop", one "Personal", one "Job", one "House and Garden", one "Vacation" ... With a tedious user switch and password prompt every time in between. But that way you could set up your music studio perfectly, and your car workshop quite differently.

In MyOS you need no users and no passwords for this, but otherwise it works quite similarly – your projects remember their workplace. And it works in perspectives, too: you are in your dark car workshop and walk over to the finance department. There you have set a light theme and immediately find word processor and spreadsheet in the taskbar. Back in the car workshop, they're gone again. Charming, isn't it?
