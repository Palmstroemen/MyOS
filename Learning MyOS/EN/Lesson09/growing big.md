Now let's assume, your little startup grows big and becomes a big company.
What have your folders now become?

| Admin | General Management |
| Info | some general information for new employees |
| Team | your HR department |
| Communication | a lot of departments like |
|  | Internal communication |
|  | Customer relations |
|  | Public relations (Website, Social Media)
|  | Investor relations |
|  | Sales |
|  | Aftersales |
| Finance | your finance department (accounting) |
| Legal | your legal department |
| Resources | Cars, Machines, Houses, ... |
| Documentation | your archive, your movies and images |

Depending on your needs you might add some additional templates, for example for accounting, customer relationship management, technical development, software development, websites, ... you name it.

We can imagine a growing marketplace for templates. There will be templates that match each other like a building block system. Companies might want to create their own templates or template systems.

As of now we provide a small set of additional templates to our standard-template.
You remember that our standard template didn't match well with persons as it is missing something like /health/ and /education/. That's exactly what our *persons-template* adds to our standard template.

Currently a team of experts in various areas of education, health, industry, governance, ... are working on the creation of a set of templates for our first release.

We believe the real power of such templates lies in a kind of standardisation. If every company develops their own templates, interchangeability might suffer. So while we allow for individualisation, we at the same time want to provide a usable set of templates ourselves.

So much about templates, individualization, standardization and a possible template-market in the future.
