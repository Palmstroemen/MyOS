Now let's assume your little startup grows and becomes a big company.
What have your folders become?

| 00_Admin | general management |
| 01_Info | general information, for example for new employees |
| 02_Team | your HR department |
| 03_Communication | a lot of departments, like |
|  | internal communication |
|  | customer relations |
|  | public relations (website, social media) |
|  | investor relations |
|  | sales |
|  | after-sales |
| 04_Finance | your finance department (accounting) |
| 05_Legal | your legal department |
| 06_Resources | vehicles, machines, buildings, ... |
| 07_Work | your production, your development – the actual work |
| 08_Documentation | your archive, your movies and pictures |

Depending on your needs, you might add more templates, for example for accounting, customer relationship management, technical development, software development, websites ... you name it.

We can imagine a growing marketplace for templates. There will be templates that fit together like the pieces of a building-block system. Companies might create their own templates or whole template systems.

As of now, we provide a small set of additional templates on top of our Standard template.
You remember: for people, our Standard template didn't quite fit, because something like health and education was missing. That's exactly what our *Person template* adds: /07_Health/ and /08_Education/.

Currently a team of experts from various fields – education, health, industry, public administration ... – is working on a set of templates for our first release.

We believe the real power of such templates lies in a certain standardization. If every company develops its own templates, interchangeability suffers. So we allow for individualization and at the same time provide a usable set of templates ourselves.

So much for templates, individualization, standardization, and a possible template marketplace in the future.
