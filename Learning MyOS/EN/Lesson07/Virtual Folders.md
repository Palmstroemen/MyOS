Let me show you some magic.
For this you need to open a classic file browser.
You can find it in the dock on the far left at the bottom of the screen. It has a blue folder-like symbol.

Open this file browser and navigate to ...
There you find a folder called 'vacation 2025' full of files.
Have a brief look at it, and exit the folder again so you see it from the outside.

Maybe you want to place this file browser to the right half of your screen, and our one to the left half of the screen so you can see them side by side.

You could also see your folder appearing in the left file browser. This might be a small project with just a few files inside. But as your small project grows you may want to add some structure to it. 
With a right-click on your folder in the left file browser you get a context menu like: "Turn into a project".

You should notice that on the left side the symbol of your folder has changed. It now shows that it has become a somehow 'enhanced' project folder.

On the right side, you'll see that the folder hasn't changed at all. This is the 'old' filesystem that doesn't know about our changes. What we do is kind of putting a thin fancy layer of magic over your operating system. On the left side you see the future of computing. On the right side the past. 
In a way it's like opening the hood of your car. On the right side, you see the motor, on the left side the car with electric windows, seat-heating, and all modern comforts.

As you understand the difference, let's have a look inside the two folders. Start with the right file browser. It should show you what you expect. A bunch of files.

Now have a look at the left file browser. It looks almost the same, with one difference. **Do you see the standard folders on top?** They aren't there yet as you can see in the right file browser. They are just virtually there. They are proposals, unborn folders, embryos.

Now in the left file browser, drag a file into one of these virtual folders. Maybe a bill into /finance/. **It's a kind of magic!**
All of a sudden, this folder got real. Now it's not semi-transparent anymore. It got born. Look at the right side! Your new folder is there, with the file inside.
##### Your advantage:
* You didn't have to create a bunch of folders manually,
* You got a bunch of standard folders for free,
* AND they are always named the same! Which makes them understandable to your computer without AI.

Now try to put all the loose files in your standard folders. For files that do not fit into one of our folders leave them outside for the moment.

There are maybe a few files left, that may not fit into our standard folders. But as you are in cleaning up mode, you might want to put these files in a folder of your own choice. So create one. We do not keep you from creating your own folders and folder structures.

We hope you now understand the principle of our standard folders. They are there for you to use as a proposal, but they are not there until you actually use them.
