When your idea grows, it often grows beyond the note where it started. You might collect images or calculations. The natural way to organize a few files is folders: just put together what belongs together. That's what we've always done, what everyone understands, and what we will do here as well.

If you have been around for a while, you might remember when operating systems suddenly came with predefined folders: *Music*, *Pictures*, *Videos*, *Downloads*, or *Documents*.
All of a sudden we knew where to put our music, pictures, and videos – and even better, the computer also knew where to look for them.

Time has passed since those days. Nowadays we don't put our music library on our computer – we stream music from the web. Photos from our phones come mixed with videos. And most videos are no longer cut into a nice movie. They have become more like moving photos with sound.

Nevertheless, our operating systems still provide the same old standard folders, which have become outdated and partly useless.

So it's time for something new. Maybe we should organize our files and documents not around the computer, but around ourselves – around our lives and the way WE organize ourselves. An operating system that puts YOU at the center of your file organization.

But how could this be done?
