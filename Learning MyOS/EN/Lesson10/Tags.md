Do you know macOS? Are you familiar with those funny colored dots for tagging files there? Did you ever understand the idea behind them?

#### Here is our take on tags.
Tags are a powerful means to organize yourself.
Different projects often need different tags. In one project you might want tags like:
* important
* urgent
* in progress
* done

In another one you might need:
* prepared
* processed
* checked
* fixed
* open

And for your finances maybe:
* not paid
* postponed
* waiting
* enforced
* paid

So projects should **contain** the flood of tags that would otherwise overwhelm your whole system. That's exactly what projects in MyOS can do.

We give you tags on steroids.
Tag what you want, whenever you want, however you want – in an easy, human-readable, yet consistent way.

And what about tags that carry a number, a weight? Something that tells you: 40 % done, 80 % done, not very important but a bit – maybe 30 % important?

You organize yourself with the Disney method or the Eisenhower matrix? Here are the means for it, right in your operating system. Not in a specialized app. In your operating system! Available to all your programs. We are sure they will learn to make use of it – and so will you.
