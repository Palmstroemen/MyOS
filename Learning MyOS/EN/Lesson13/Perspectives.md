Now the full potential of our standard folders unfolds.

Imagine your mail program, your chat program knows that it is responsible for communication. So it files your mails under /03_Communication/ and not, say, under /08_Documentation/ or /01_Info/.
And it no longer stuffs your mails into one huge folder of its own, but into many small folders inside YOUR projects! After all, they are YOUR mails, not its mails. And YOU want them where they belong.

Unfortunately, we are not quite there yet. But we have prepared everything so that software developers can now implement this way of filing quickly and easily. The view that makes it possible, we call *perspectives*.

Let's explain perspectives with a picture. Imagine a special company: its departments (your projects) are arranged around a central administration building. In it sit the finance department, the legal department, marketing and sales, the archive, and so on. Each of your projects has direct access to all these departments – these are the folders you already know – and can file its things there.
The finance department, in turn, sees all these documents (from all projects it has access to) – but sorted by projects and subprojects.

Technically speaking,
/Projects/ProjectXY/Subproject5/**04_Finance**/...   becomes
/**04_Finance**/Projects/ProjectXY/Subproject5/...

That's a perspective, and you can take it at any time, very easily. Just as well:
/Projects/ProjectXY/Subproject5/**04_Finance/Expenses**/...   becomes
/**04_Finance/Expenses**/Projects/ProjectXY/Subproject5/...

So you can take care of the expenses in the finance department and still have direct access to all your projects.

Think about what that means! In your project you have your standard folders (/04_Finance/, /01_Info/ ...), and when you switch to the finance department, your folders lie before you sorted by projects (/ProjectA/, /ProjectB/ ...).

Think of your vacation photos, which from now on you file under /Vacations/Morocco 2025/08_Documentation/Pictures/.
You might say: that would be terrible, then my pictures are scattered everywhere and no longer in one place. I want it like in my Pictures folder!
That's exactly what the perspective /08_Documentation/Pictures/ does for you. There you see all your photos, sorted by projects. Only you don't have to laboriously rebuild your whole project structure by hand there, as you have to in every operating system today.

Have you rebuilt your project structure in your mail program, too? In your accounting, your task manager, on your SharePoint, in your Nextcloud? Do you see what this means? ONE project structure, ALWAYS the same everywhere, without rebuilding and maintaining it again and again. All documents of a project always exactly where they belong: with the project. Copied onto a USB stick and handed over – and the other person has everything and finds their way around at once.
And at the same time overviews of all invoices, all photos, all videos, all contracts ...

And this already works in MyOS today. In any of your programs, you can take the way through a perspective when you save a document.
