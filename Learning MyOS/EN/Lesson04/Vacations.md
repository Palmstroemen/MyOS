But before we do that, let's go on vacation ...
... hmm. Turns out a vacation is quite a project.
1. First you collect information about where you'd like to go.
2. Then you decide.
3. Then you book flights and hotels.
4. Maybe you talk to a travel agency, or to friends who want to join in.
5. Maybe you rent a car as well.
6. Then there are insurance policies for flights, hotels, and the rental car.
7. Finally you're on vacation. But what happens there?
8. You take a bunch of photos.
9. Maybe you bring all your invoices and receipts back home, and you do ...
10. ... a final calculation of the costs.

The documents of this vacation lie right here, next to this note. Now leave this note for a moment and try to organize them into useful folders that you create yourself.
Start with a folder for your vacation, something like "vacation summer 2026", and inside it create new folders for the things you want to group.
