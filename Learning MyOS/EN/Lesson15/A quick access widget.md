By the way: press **Ctrl + Alt + Space**. The **folder fan** appears right at your mouse – a new kind of tool to find a folder very quickly.

Move the mouse outward, and you dive deeper into your folders, level by level. Once you have found the right one, you can
* drop files right onto it, or
* double-click to go there.

Then the fan disappears again. It is there for exactly one action – and then it's gone.

Try it! The folder fan is still in development, but here you can try a little preview already. You set how it behaves in Scope's setup (⚙, SunTree tab). Here, too, we look forward to your feedback.
