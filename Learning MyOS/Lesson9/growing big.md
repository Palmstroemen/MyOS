Depending on your needs you might add up some additional Templates like for accounting, customers relation management, technical development, software development, websites, ... you name it.

We can imagine a growing marketplace for templates. There will be templates that match to each other like a building block system. Companies might want to create their own templates oder template-systems.

As of now we provide a small set of additional templates to our standard-template.
You remember that our standard template didn't match well with persons as it is missing something like /health/ and /education/. That's exactly, what our *persons-template* add to our standard-template.

Currently a team of experts in various areas of education, health, industry, governance, ... are working on the creation of a set of templates four our first release.

We belive the real power of sucht templates lies in some kind of standardisation. If every company develops their own templates, interchangeability might suffer. So while we allow for individualization, we at the same time want to provide a usable set of templates by ourselfs.

So much about templates, individualization, standardization and a possible template-market in the future.
