When your idea grows, it often grows beyond the original note where it started. You might collect images or calculations. The natural way to organize a few files is to put them into folders. That's what we've always done, what everyone understands, and what we will do here as well.

Some of you who have been around for a while might remember when operating systems suddenly came with predefined folders like Music, Pictures, Videos, Downloads, or Documents.
All of a sudden we knew where to put our music, pictures, and videos — and even better, the computer also knew where to look for them.

Time has passed since those days. Nowadays we don't put our music library on our computer — we stream music from the web. When importing photos from our phones, they come mixed with videos. Most videos are no longer meant to be cut into a nice movie. They have become more like moving photos with sound.

Nevertheless, our operating systems still provide the same old standard folders, which have become outdated and partly useless.

So it's time for something new. Maybe we should organize our files and documents not around the computer, but around ourselves — an operating system that puts YOU, the user, at the center of your file organization.
