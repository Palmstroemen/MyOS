Let me show you some magic.
For this you need to open a classic file browser.
You can find it in the dock on the far left at the bottom of the screen. It has a blue folder-like symbol.

Open this file browser and navigate to ...
There you find a folder called 'vacation 2025' full of files.
Have a brief look at it, and exit the folder again so you see it from the outside.

Maybe you want to place this file browser to the right half of your screen, and our one to the left half of the screenn so you can see them side by side.

You yould also see your Folder appearing in the left file browser. This might be a small project with just a few files inside. But as your small project grows you may want to add some structure to it. 
With a right-click on your folder in the left file-browser you get a context menuy like: "turn to a project".

You should remark that on the left side the symbol of your folder has changed. It now shows that it's become an somehow 'enhanced' project folder.

On the right side, you'll see that the folder hasn't changed at all. This is somehow the 'old'-Filesystem that doesn't know about our changes. What we do is kind of putting a thin fancy layer of magic over your operating-system. On the left side you see the future of computing. On the right side the past. 
In a way it's like opening the Motorhaube of your car. On the right side, you see the motor, on the left side the car with electric windows, seat-heating, an all modern comfort.

As you understand the differenc, let's have a look inside the two folders. Start with the right file-browser. It should show you what you expect. A bunch of files.

Now have a look at the left file-browser. It looks almost the same, with one difference. **Do you see the standard-folders on top?** They are'nt there yet as you can see on the right side file-browser. They are just virtually there. They are proposals, unborn Folders, Embryos.

Now in the left file-browser pull a file into one of these virtual folders. Maybe a bill into /finance/. **It's a kind of magic!**
All of a sudden, this folder got real. Now it's not semi-transparent anymore. It got born. Look at the right side! Your new folder is there, with the file inside.
##### Your advantage:
* You didn't have to create a bunch of folders manually,
* You got a bunch of standard-folders for free,
* AND they are always named the same! Which makes them understandable to your computer withoug AI.

Now try to put all the loose files in your standard folders. For files that do not fit into one of our folders leave them outside for the moment.

There are maybe a few files left, that may not fit into our standard folders. But as you are in cleaning up mode, you might want to put these files in a folder of your own choice. So create one. We do not keep you from creating your own folders and folder-structures.

We hope, now you got the principle idea of our standard folders. They are there for you to be used as a proposal, but the are not ther until you actually use them.
